Data & maps

Tables, charts & maps.

Data visualisation is deliberately minimalist — only the essentials, always led by the primary blues. Tables use Dark Blue headers and 0.5pt dividers; charts sequence color by priority; maps stay clean, with offices always marked by yellow dots.
