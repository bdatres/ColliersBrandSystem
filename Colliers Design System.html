01 — Brand & voice

Confident, credible, human.

Colliers is a global commercial real estate services and investment management firm. The voice is enterprising and expert — grounded in data, written in plain English, always centerd on the client. "We" for Colliers, "you" for the client, and a confident closing line.

Do
  • "Your success is our success."
  • "We act quickly to create and seize opportunities."
  • Plain English. Active voice. Short sentences.
  • One closing assertion: "At Colliers, we accelerate success."
Don't
  • "Industry-leading synergistic outcomes."
  • "Stakeholder-aligned ROI maximization."
  • Jargon, hedging, buzzwords, passive voice.
  • Emoji, exclamation marks, or breathless adjectives.

Five brand values underpin everything: be enterprising, collaborate, invest in relationships, be experts, do what's right.

03 — Typography

Open Sans, warmed by Merriweather.

Open Sans is the workhorse — Light for headlines, Regular for body, Semibold for emphasis, Bold for all-caps section labels. Merriweather adds editorial warmth in subheads and pull quotes. Never mix the two on a single line.

07 — Blocks & keylines

The signature composition.

Solid primary-blue blocks and thin dark-blue keylines are the brand's most recognisable graphic device. At most two blocks per layout; if two, they overlap with a ≥ 3:1 size ratio. Keylines frame and organize, and the tagline locks into them.

Blocks + keyline — how they interact

Overlapping — a keyline may overlap a block, as long as it isn't the same color/tint as the block beneath.

Gap space — the keyline breaks before and after a block it crosses; the gap equals half the layout margin.

Frame color — a single frame can hold one color or switch as it leads into a darker / lighter background or photo.

08 — Data & maps

Tables, charts & maps.

The master brand mark.

The full-color logo is used on every digital surface. Minimum 80px wide on screen; clear space equals 2× the height of the color bars on all sides. Never recolor, rotate, add shadows, or place on a colored background without a white keyline.
