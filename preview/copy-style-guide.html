Copy style guide — quick tips

Grammatical and style errors diminish our credibility and professionalism. U.S. content follows this guide first, then the current AP Stylebook for anything not covered — always U.S. English (color, not color).

Colliers' possessive

Colliers'

Collier's

Advisor, not adviser

advisor

adviser

Square feet

sq. ft. in body · SF in charts/ads

sq ft / sq.ft

Job title casing

Jane Smith, managing director

Cap only immediately before a name

Business lines & teams

Roberts Debt Advisory Group

Always capitalized

Services & sectors

…retail, office and industrial sectors.

Lowercase unless part of a team name

Million / billion

$500 million · $500B (tables/headlines)

Spell out in body copy

Numbers

one–nine spelled out, 10+ numeric

Mixed styles in one piece

Casing

Sentence case is readable.

No Title Case or ALL CAPS, even in lists

Dates

June 21, 2019 · June 21

Never 21st, 21rd, etc.

Internet terms

email · online · website

e-mail · on-line · web-site

"&" usage

Engineering & Design (service line names)

"&" in body copy
