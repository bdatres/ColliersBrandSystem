## Quick reference

- **Tagline:** "Accelerating success." — sentence case, never standalone.
- **Color rule:** one of Deep Blue (#000759), Dark Blue (#25408F) or Medium Blue (#1C54F4) must dominate every design.
- **Type:** Open Sans (primary), Merriweather (warm/editorial). Never mix on the same line.
- **Eyebrow style:** Open Sans Bold, ALL CAPS, tracking +0.18em.
- **Highlight box** (rounded medium-blue box around an emphasis word) is the signature type treatment — it nods to the logo's rounded rectangle.
- **Logo stripe** (yellow / cyan / red bar) is a brand accent — keep all three colors together.
- **No emoji.** No gradients in body. No drop shadows on the logo.
