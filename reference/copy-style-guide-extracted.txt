The Purpose
of this Guide
Everything we do and write contributes to Colliers’ reputation.
Grammatical and style errors diminish our credibility and
professionalism. This can lead to tangible, negative outcomes –
editors could reject our media releases, our proposals for new
business might be unsuccessful, or readers might misunderstand
what we mean. Conversely, cleanly edited, accurate and succinctly
compelling content helps readers immediately understand,
engage and respond effectively to our messages while
contributing to Colliers brand success.
We have put together the following guidelines to help ensure
that all internal and external communications are on brand and
consistent in the market. For a comprehensive reference library
to help you communicate successfully, follow this guide for
work in the U.S. and Colliers’ global style guide for international
communications or where a question arises that is not covered by
the U.S. guide.
Keep this guide handy as a grammar, punctuation and style
reference to help you write consistently in the Colliers voice.
