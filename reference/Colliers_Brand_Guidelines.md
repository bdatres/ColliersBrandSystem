## Brand Story

### Mission

> Maximize the potential of property and real assets to accelerate the success of our clients, our investors and our people.

### Our Values

**Be enterprising to exceed expectations.**
For our people: Engaged and empowered to make quick, informed decisions that help you and your clients grow and thrive. For our clients: We act quickly to create and seize opportunities, embracing change and thinking differently to generate new and better solutions.

**Collaborate to drive exceptional results.**
For our people: Work with and learn from experts to meet client needs, in your market and beyond. For our clients: We build teams within and across markets with the right expertise, market experience and connections to deliver the right solutions.

**Invest in relationships to deliver enduring value.**
For our people: Opportunity to build true partnerships with colleagues and clients for long-term success. For our clients: We are a long-term partner, looking beyond immediate needs to build and grow our clients' business.

**Be experts to lead the industry into the future.**
For our people: At the forefront of your field, constantly taking on new challenges and honing new skills. For our clients: At the forefront of the real estate industry, backed by an exceptional record of success.

**Do what's right for our clients, people and communities.**
For our people: Respectful and caring, holding yourself and others to the highest ethical standards. For our clients: Integrity is essential to Colliers; the trust we develop internally sets the tone for external relationships.

### Narrative

We are enterprising. Our expert advice to property occupiers, owners and investors leads the industry into the future. We invest in relationships to create enduring value.

What sets us apart is not what we do, but how we do it. Our people are passionate, take personal responsibility and always do what's right for our clients, people and communities. We attract and develop industry leaders, empowering them to think and act differently to drive exceptional results.

What's more, our global reach maximizes the potential of property, wherever our clients do business.

At Colliers, we accelerate success.

### Value Proposition

**Employee:** Our enterprising culture empowers our people to do their best work, collaborating with our global experts to maximize every opportunity and lead the industry.

**Client:** Your success is our success; we are dedicated to driving exceptional results with the right property solutions for you, wherever you operate.

### Brand Voice

Our voice is **confident, credible, passionate, human, and authentic** — never dry, arrogant, condescending, aggressive, or disingenuous.

- **Confident:** Grounded in knowledge, experience and data; always willing to be bold and take smart risks.
- **Human:** Real words, clear language, honest and not unnecessarily formal.
- **Passionate:** We take our work personally; making client success our top priority.

## Logo

### Primary Logo

The Colliers logo is defined by a prominent use of blue and an impactful colour bar accent. The full-colour logo should be used on all applications.

### Clear Space & Position

- Minimum clear space equals **2x the height of the colour bars** from the white keyline on all sides.
- Ideally placed in a corner of the composition, away from key messaging. May be centred if aligned with text.

### Minimum Size

- Print: at least **0.5" / 13mm** wide
- Screen: at least **80px** wide

### Logo Versions & Usage

| Version | When to Use |
|---|---|
| Full colour | All print and digital materials — always preferred |
| Co-branded lockup | Only when approved by partner organization and Regional Brand Lead |
| Single colour / black | Where multiple colours are not supported (silkscreen, embossing, etching, etc.) |
| Black only | Reception signs, embroidery, printing with size constraints |
| Wordmark only | Strict production limitations and reception signs only |

### Tagline

"Accelerating success." is always set in **Open Sans Regular, sentence case**. It can be locked up with the logo or used separately only if the logo appears elsewhere in the design. It cannot stand alone.

- Use on primary or secondary palette backgrounds only.
- Do not use the tertiary palette for the tagline.

### File Formats

| Format | Best For |
|---|---|
| EPS | Print and sign applications (vector, scalable) |
| JPEG | Microsoft Word, PowerPoint, digital media with white backgrounds |
| PNG | Digital media with non-white or transparent backgrounds |

### Logo Don'ts

- Do not stretch, compress, rotate or add transparency
- Do not colorize or add drop shadows, gradients or outlines
- Do not use on coloured backgrounds or photos without a white keyline
- Do not use JPEG version on coloured or photographic backgrounds
- Do not use special use logos when the full-colour logo can be used
- Do not add text beneath or above the logo
- Do not change any elements

### Colour Balance

Select one primary blue to dominate the design, using remaining colours (including white) to balance. Highly saturated colours like Medium Blue require more white or light colours to balance. Avoid compositions without a dominant colour or sufficient contrast.

## Typography

### Primary Typeface: Open Sans

Most copy should be set in Open Sans. If unavailable, use **Verdana** as a fallback (email, etc.).

| Weight | Best For |
|---|---|
| Open Sans Light | Headlines at large point sizes; not effective at small sizes |
| Open Sans Regular | Subheads, body copy, longer sections |
| Open Sans Semibold | Selective emphasis, headlines, very small sizes |
| Open Sans Bold | Emphasis in body copy, charts, document type labels — use minimally |
| Open Sans Condensed Light/Bold | Legal copy, footnotes, data-heavy tables |

### Secondary Typeface: Merriweather

Adds warmth and approachability. Use for subheads, pull quotes and body copy in long-format reports. Can be used in headlines when a more authoritative tone is needed. Never mix with Open Sans in headlines or on a single line. If unavailable, use **Georgia** as a fallback.

| Weight | Best For |
|---|---|
| Merriweather Light | Large headlines, graphic editorial elements |
| Merriweather Regular | Pull quotes, subheadings paired with Open Sans Semibold |
| Merriweather Bold | Sparingly, smaller sizes for emphasis or contrast |

### Type Hierarchy

| Style | Spec |
|---|---|
| Document/Section Heading | Open Sans Bold, all caps, tracking 100–175 |
| Headline | Open Sans Light |
| Subhead | Merriweather Regular |
| Body Copy | Open Sans Regular |
| Body Copy CTA | Open Sans Bold |

### Headline Emphasis

Use no more than one or two words of emphasis per sentence. Never combine more than one type of emphasis (e.g., do not use both colour and weight shift in the same sentence).

- **Colour Emphasis:** Use two steps brighter or darker for the emphasized word.
- **Weight Emphasis:** Use two steps heavier than the body weight (e.g., Open Sans Light + Open Sans Semibold).
- **Colour Underscore:** Stroke weight should match the visual weight of the text; colour must differ from the typeface colour.
- **Colour Highlight:** A nod to the rounded corners of the Colliers logo. Medium Blue preferred for digital; Deep Blue or Dark Blue for print. Type within the highlight box must always be white.

### Accessibility (WCAG 2.0)

Typesetting for web must meet WCAG 2.0 standards. Contrast thresholds vary with text weight and size — verify specific use cases with online accessibility tools. Black text may only be used when branding is unnecessary (internal memos, standard email, etc.).

## Graphic Elements

### Blocks

- **Maximum of two blocks** per layout.
- When two blocks are used, they **must overlap**; one block should be noticeably larger (ratio of at least 3:1).
- Single blocks can be anchored to the edge or float anywhere on the canvas.
- Opacity can be reduced to 95% when overlaid on a photo or another block. Do not use Multiply blending mode.
- Gap between block and keyline is optional but should equal half the margin width.

#### Block Colour Rules

- Use only **primary blues** for blocks: Deep Blue, Colliers Dark Blue, Medium Blue.
- The accent block should be two or three steps brighter or darker, or white.
- Secondary and tertiary palette colours **cannot** be used for blocks.
- Do **not** use Colliers Light Blue (from the logo stripe) as a colour block.
- Insufficient contrast combinations are not permitted.

### Keyline

The keyline frames, organizes or connects elements and guides the viewer's attention to the primary message. Seven usage options are available — select the one that best suits the composition.

#### Keyline Colour

Best colours are the three darker blues: Deep Blue, Dark Blue and Medium Blue. Light Blue can be used where colour contrast is sufficient on very light images.

#### Keyline Gradient

Three gradient colour options: Medium Blue, Light Blue and Deep Blue — always paired with Pale Blue Grey or White as the lightest colour.

- **Frames:** Begin and end with the darker colour; Pale Blue Grey highlight in the centre.
- **Linear extending highlight:** Transition from darker colour to Pale Blue Grey.
- **Gradient position:** 20%–60% is recommended for smooth transitions.

#### Keyline + Tagline

When using a framed, left/right aligned or encompassing keyline, the tagline should intersect the keyline. For organizing, extending and connecting keylines, the tagline should appear at the bottom right or left of the composition. The tagline may only be placed along the bottom of the frame or in the logo lockup.

### Icons

Colliers branded icons use an outline style with small, occasional line breaks that connect to the keyline element.

- Always use the **primary colour palette** for icons.
- Do not use secondary or tertiary colours.
- Do not use more than one colour per icon.
- Stroke weight should appear uniformly at 1pt (scaled proportionately for large formats).
- For digital interactive elements, use an alternative blue shade or white for hover states.

## Tables, Charts & Maps

### Tables

Tables should be clean, simple and easy to scan.

**Rows and Columns**
Use 0.5pt dividers or alternating shades of Light Grey or Extra Light Blue (at 40% opacity) to separate rows. Only use vertical dividers if column separation is not immediately clear.

**Colours**
Stick to the primary and secondary palettes. Use darker colours for clear headers and section dividers.

**Fonts**
Tables use Open Sans, typically at 9pt in Colliers Dark Blue or Dark Blue Grey for data, and 9pt in White, Pale Blue or Light Blue for headers. Open Sans Condensed Light and Bold can be used for footnotes, legal copy and dense data. All caps titles and headers must be set in Open Sans Bold with at least +50 tracking.

**Alignment**
Body copy: left-aligned. Numbers with decimals: right-aligned. Numbers without decimals (percentages, page numbers): may be centre-aligned.

**Dividers**
Dividers should be 0.5pt.

---

### Charts

Chart design should be minimalist — include only the essentials. Remove what's not important to the data being presented.

**Labels**
Use Open Sans or Open Sans Condensed. Most labels work best in all caps Open Sans Bold with at least +50 tracking.

**Colour Priority**
Use primary colours first in this order: Deep Blue, Dark Blue, Medium Blue, Light Blue, Pale Blue. Expand into secondary and tertiary palettes only when additional differentiation is needed.

Tints for further differentiation: 80%, 60%, 40%, 20%, 10%.

**Note on Red**
Given red's association with negative trends or decline, carefully consider the implications before using Colliers Red in charts where the data does not represent a negative or declining trend.

### Maps

Map design should be minimalist and clean, consistent with the overall visual identity.

**Coverage Maps — Approved Colour Combinations**

1. Pale Blue Grey on White
2. Grey on White
3. Light Blue Grey on White
4. Light Blue Grey on Pale Blue Grey

Maps can be set at 80%, 60%, 40%, 20% or 10% opacity for subtler applications. Border lines should be 0.25pt at 50% of the landmass colour.

**Textured Maps**
Simple graphic textures (lines or dots) can be applied for visual interest. Ensure the texture is dense enough that important map details are not lost.

**Dot Style**
Dot diameter: 0.07"–0.1" (2–3mm). Use 5pt Open Sans Bold for labels inside dots. Smaller dots generally work better on zoomed-out maps.

**Additional Markers**
Always use yellow dots for Colliers offices. Other colours and markers should complement the existing style. When a range of highlight colours is used, a Deep Blue stroke (instead of white) can provide sufficient contrast.

**Sub Regions**
Use tints of primary blues at 100%, 80%, 60%, 40%, 20% and 10% to separate regions. Ensure sufficient contrast between regions.

**Property Location Maps**
Highlight key amenities and major streets relevant to the audience. Add location names only when they add value (e.g., recognizable hotel or restaurant brands). Include simple icons to indicate amenity types. Use the primary and secondary palettes in a combination that best suits the overall composition.
